# Antislop audit 001 (2026-11-24)

2. **HIGH, R-03:** `src/components/app/AppShell.tsx` account-menu trigger uses the small button size in the mobile header. Its default height is less than the required 44px touch target. Give the trigger a minimum height of 44px.
