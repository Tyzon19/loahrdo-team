# Certificate of Employment

## Project overview

The HRDO Certificate of Employment system lets Lyceum of Alabang employees request a certificate, follow its progress, and receive it through the delivery option they selected. HRDO staff review the employee's information, prepare or upload the signed certificate, and record when the document is ready or released.

## How a request moves through the system

1. An employee signs in to the HRDO service and submits a request, including its purpose and whether a compensation review is needed.
2. HRDO reviews the submitted information. Staff can verify the details, begin processing, ask the employee to correct information, or reject the request with remarks.
3. HRDO prepares the certificate using the approved template or uploads the signed document.
4. Staff mark the request ready and record the recipient when the document is released. The employee can check the request status and history along the way.

Requests are expected to be processed within three days of submission. The dashboard highlights requests approaching their deadline.

## Certificate and delivery options

Employees may request a soft copy, an original hard copy, or both. The system supports signed PDF and Word documents, with a 5 MB upload limit. If the request includes compensation information, the generated certificate can show the amount in figures and words. Compensation is included only when the employee selects that request option.

For hardcopy-only requests, HRDO records the release for pickup. If a soft copy is requested, the system can send the prepared certificate electronically when the workflow reaches release.

## Access and responsibilities

- **Employee:** submits a request, responds to correction requests, and tracks its status.
- **HRDO staff:** reviews and verifies details, prepares or uploads the certificate, and records readiness and release.
- **System administrator:** creates the first HRDO administrator from the main application’s Profile page under **HRDO processors**. An HRDO administrator can then create processor accounts from `/hrdo/profile`. Each account uses an institutional email, signs in through `/hrdo/login`, and must change its temporary password at first sign-in.

The HRDO service has its own employee and staff accounts, sessions, and database tables. Its sign-in is separate from the vehicle reservation and trip-ticket system, even though both services are part of this project.
