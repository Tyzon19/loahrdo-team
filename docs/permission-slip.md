---
title: Permission Slip
description: Submit, review, approve, and archive campus departure permission slips.
---

The Permission Slip system lets Lyceum of Alabang employees request authorization to leave campus.
Employees submit their department, schedule, and reason. Assigned signatories review and decide on each request.

## User roles

- **Employee:** submits Permission Slips and views submitted and archived slips.
- **Department signatory:** reviews pending slips assigned to the employee's department and approves or rejects them.
- **Administrator:** manages signatories and system access.

## Submit a Permission Slip

1. Open `/permission-slips/login` and sign in with the account assigned for Permission Slip access.
2. Open **Request Permission Slip**.
3. Enter the department or office.
4. Enter the departure date, departure time, and return time.
5. State the reason for leaving campus.
6. Add an electronic signature.
7. Review the details and submit the request.

Department information is saved on the employee's request. Employees can update their name and department in **Profile & account**.

## Review a request

Assigned signatories open **Permission Slip Review** to view pending requests.

For each slip, the signatory can:

- Approve the request.
- Reject the request with a reason.

Only assigned Permission Slip signatories can approve or reject slips.

## Status and history

Employees can open **My Permission Slips** to track requests and review their history. Each slip retains its approval or rejection status and history.

## Account setup

Permission Slip accounts use the shared institutional account system. Super Administrators can assign eligible institutional accounts as Permission Slip signatories.

Signatories must use an `@lyceumalabang.edu.ph` account. New accounts may require an initial password change before access is allowed.
