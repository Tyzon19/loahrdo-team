This page is Part VIII of the technical documentation. It covers the standard operating procedures for four groups: employees (requisitioners), approvers and signatories, HRDO processors, and system administrators.

## Employee manual

### Register a new institutional account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the registration page">
    Go to `/register`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request an OTP">
    Enter your `@lyceumalabang.edu.ph` email address, your full name, department, and a password that meets the policy (12-128 characters with lowercase, uppercase, number, and symbol). Click **Request verification code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the OTP">
    Check your email for a six-digit code. It expires in 10 minutes and can be resent after 60 seconds. Enter the code within 5 attempts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete registration">
    After the code is accepted, your requisitioner account is created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">
    Go to `/login`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a method">
    Sign in with your institutional email and password, or click **Sign in with Google** if your `@lyceumalabang.edu.ph` requisitioner account already exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle forced password changes">
    If the system shows a password-change screen, create a new password that meets the policy and continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile if needed">
    First-time Google sign-ins may be asked to confirm their name at `/complete-profile` before reaching the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Change your password

1. Sign in and open `/change-password`.
2. Enter your current password and a new password that meets the policy.
3. Submit. The system invalidates existing sessions and refreshes your login state.

### File a vehicle request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request form">
    From the dashboard, choose **Vehicle Reservation**, or go directly to `/requests/new`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter department, destination(s), number of passengers, time needed, start date, end date, and purpose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Review the form and submit. The request enters the workflow at the Head of Transport Services review stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the request">
    Open `/requests` to view all your requests, or `/requests/$id` for a specific request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Track and revise a returned request

- If a request is returned, you will receive a notification and an email.
- Open `/requests/$id` to read the return reason.
- Click **Revise request** to open `/revise/$id`.
- Edit the allowed fields and resubmit. The request restarts at the Head of Transport Services stage; previous signatures are kept separately.

### Receive the trip ticket

- After final approval, the request status becomes **approved**.
- Open `/print/$id` to preview the official trip ticket and its QR verification code.
- If the ticket was not sent, an authorized sender can resend it from the same page.
- Save or print the ticket for the trip.

### Submit a Permission Slip

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to `/permission-slips/login` and sign in with your Permission Slip account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the request form">
    From the Permission Slip dashboard, choose **Request Permission Slip**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter department, departure date, departure time, return time, reason (at least 5 characters, at most 500), and add an electronic signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Review and submit. The slip status becomes **pending_approval**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track">
    Open **My Permission Slips** to view your slips and their status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Request a Certificate of Employment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the portal">
    Current employees sign in through `/hrdo/login`. Former employees or external verifiers use the external COE flow at `/coe` with a personal Google account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the request">
    Go to `/hrdo/coe`. Enter the purpose, copy type (soft, original, or both), and whether compensation details should be included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    The request status becomes **pending**. HRDO has three working days to process it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to corrections">
    If HRDO requests a correction, open the request at `/hrdo/coe/$id`, read the message, update the required fields, and resubmit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pickup or receive the document">
    When the status becomes **ready_for_pickup** or **released**, follow the delivery instructions shown on the request page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### View attendance and flag a discrepancy

1. Sign in and open `/leaves`.
2. Review your leave balances, attendance logs, lates, and absences for the current June-to-May cycle.
3. If a record looks incorrect, select the log and click **Flag discrepancy**.
4. Enter a concise explanation. The flag is sent to HRDO for resolution.

### Notifications

- In-app notifications appear at `/notifications`. Click a notification to mark it read.
- Email status for each notification is shown as **pending**, **sent**, or **failed**.
- If email delivery fails, the in-app notification and the underlying record remain intact.

## Approver and signatory manual

### Vehicle request approval

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pending queue">
    Sign in and go to `/dashboard`. The pending count for your stage is shown in the stats card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the request">
    Open `/requests/$id`. Review dates, destinations, passengers, purpose, and prior signatures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record vehicle and driver (Head of Transport only)">
    During the transport review stage, select the assigned vehicle and driver before approving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Click **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Return**. Returns require a reason. Rejections also require a reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **Director:** reviews at the recommendation stage (`pending_recommendation`).
- **President:** reviews at the final approval stage (`pending_approval`).
- **Alternates:** assigned alternate signatories can act only within their assigned stage and sign with their own identity.

### Permission Slip approval

1. Sign in and open `/permission-slips/$id` for a pending slip, or view the pending list from the Permission Slip dashboard.
2. Review the employee name, department, schedule, reason, and signature.
3. Click **Approve** or **Reject**. A rejection requires a reason.
4. After approval, a receipt is generated. The signatory can trigger email delivery of the receipt if needed.

## HRDO Processor manual

### COE queue and review

1. Sign in at `/hrdo/login`.
2. Open `/hrdo/dashboard` to see the COE queue summary: pending, under review, correction requested, processing, awaiting review, ready for pickup, released, and rejected.
3. Click a request to open `/hrdo/coe/$id`.

### Verify and process a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Click **Verify** to confirm the employee details and purpose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start processing">
    Click **Start processing** to move the status to **processing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the document">
    Use **Generate template** to create a draft, or upload a signed file directly. Uploads must be PDF or DOCX and no larger than 5 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a correction">
    If information is incomplete or incorrect, click **Request correction** and explain what the employee should fix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reject">
    If the request cannot be fulfilled, click **Reject** and provide a reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Mark ready and release

1. After the signed document is uploaded, click **Mark ready for pickup**.
2. When the employee collects the document, or when you send the soft copy, click **Release** and record the recipient name.
3. For soft-copy or both-copy requests, the system can email the signed document when the release step is completed.

### Analytics

- Open the analytics panel on the HRDO dashboard to view summary counts, overdue requests, and due-soon flags based on the three-day deadline.

## Administrator manual

### First sign-in and initial setup

1. After `npm run db:seed`, the initial administrator account is `admin@loa.edu.ph`. The seed script prints the temporary password once.
2. Sign in at `/login` and change the password immediately.
3. From `/profile`, create the Head of Transport Services, Director for Administration, and President accounts. Each receives a temporary password by email and must change it at first sign-in.

### Create signatory accounts and assign roles

- **Main signatories:** In `/profile`, under **Signatory settings**, assign existing users to main approval stages (Head of Transport, Director, President). You can also create a new main signatory account.
- **Alternate assistants:** In the same section, assign alternate assistants per stage. Alternates can approve or return on behalf of the stage when the main signatory is unavailable.

### Phone directory

- Open `/phone-directory` to view and update contact numbers for signatories and staff.
- Save changes with `saveSignatoryPhoneNumberFn`.

### Manage vehicles

- Go to `/vehicles`.
- Add a vehicle with the driver's name, vehicle type, and plate number.
- Update or delete vehicle records as needed. Existing request history is preserved.

### Reset passwords

- In `/profile`, under **Account management**, select a user and reset their password.
- The system emails a temporary password. The user must change it at next sign-in.
- For HRDO processors, use the HRDO processor settings in `/profile` to reset passwords.

### Receipts archive and bulk ZIP

- Go to `/receipts`.
- Filter by system, date range, and status.
- Select receipts and click **Download ZIP** to bulk download a ZIP archive of the selected files.
- Preview individual receipts before downloading.

### Trip archive and analytics

- Go to `/trip-archive`.
- Search by ticket number, destination, requester, driver, or vehicle.
- Filter by travel dates.
- Review trip counts and destination analytics.

### Late and absences sync and mappings

- Go to `/admin/late-absences`.
- Choose a staff group: NON_TEACHING, COLLEGE, or BASIC_ED.
- Click **Sync** to pull data from the configured Google Sheet.
- Review employee mappings and correct mismatched names with **Save mapping**.
- Resolve discrepancy flags raised by employees.

### Super Admin system access

- Super Admins can open `/admin` to view the system overview, module totals, recent users, and access activity.
- Use **System access settings** in `/profile` to assign or remove system access roles.
- Super Admins can manage HRDO processor accounts and Permission Slip signatories.
