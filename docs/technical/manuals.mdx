# Employee manual

## Sign in

1. Open the LOA HRDO Desk service dashboard.
2. Select the required service.
3. Sign in with an institutional or eligible account.
4. Complete a forced password change if prompted.
5. Update the profile if required.

## Vehicle reservation

1. Open **Vehicle Reservation**.
2. Enter trip dates, time, passengers, destination, and purpose.
3. Review the request and submit it.
4. Track the request from My Requests.
5. Edit or resubmit if it is returned.
6. Download or receive the official ticket after final approval.
7. Use the public verification link printed on the official ticket when needed.

## Certificate of Employment

1. Open **COE Request**.
2. Choose internal employee or former employee or verifier.
3. Sign in using the applicable account method.
4. Enter purpose, copy type, and compensation details if applicable.
5. Submit the request.
6. Review status and respond to correction requests.
7. Receive or collect the final document according to delivery status.

## Permission Slip

1. Open `/permission-slips/login`.
2. Sign in or complete the required password change.
3. Open **Request Permission Slip**.
4. Enter department, date, departure time, return time, reason, and signature.
5. Review and submit.
6. Open **My Permission Slips** to track the request.

## Late and Absences

Open **Late & Absences** to view leave balances, attendance logs, lates, absences, staff mappings, and discrepancy status. Submit a discrepancy with a concise explanation when an attendance record is incorrect.

# Approver manual

## Vehicle approval

Approvers open the pending request queue for their assigned stage. Review request details, add remarks or signature, approve, reject, or return the request with a reason.

Transport Heads assign an approved vehicle and driver. The Director and President complete their configured stages. Alternates act only within assigned stage authorization.

## Permission Slip approval

Assigned signatories open **Permission Slip Review**. Review the employee, department, date, time, reason, and signature. Approve or reject the slip with the required decision information.

# Administrator manual

## Account management

1. Open the central admin dashboard.
2. Review existing users and module access.
3. Create or assign authorized accounts.
4. Set temporary passwords or reset credentials.
5. Remove or reassign signatory access when organizational responsibility changes.

## Records and reports

Administrators can search trip archives, Permission Slip history, COE records, attendance records, notifications, and available receipts according to role.

## HRDO operations

HRDO administrators manage processor accounts and review COE workloads. HRDO staff verify requests, prepare documents, request corrections, mark documents ready, and record release or pickup.
