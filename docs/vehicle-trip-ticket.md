# Vehicle Trip Ticket

## Project overview

The Vehicle Reservation and Trip Ticket system helps Lyceum of Alabang staff request campus vehicles and gives the Physical Facilities Management Office a clear record of each approved trip. Requesters submit trip details online, approvers review the request in sequence, and the completed ticket can be downloaded and presented to the driver.

## How a request moves through the system

1. A requester signs in with an approved institutional account and submits a vehicle request with the trip dates, destination, purpose, and passenger details.
2. The Head of Transport Services reviews the request and records the assigned vehicle and driver.
3. The Director for Administration reviews the request, followed by the President.
4. Once fully approved, the authorized staff member sends the official trip ticket to the requester. The requester can open and download it from the request page.
5. The official ticket includes a QR code that links to a public verification page. The page checks the ticket against the approved request record.

## Records and oversight

Approved tickets remain in the trip archive and can be searched by ticket number, destination, requester, driver, or vehicle. Staff can filter trips by travel dates and review activity totals. Completed requests count as actual trips; approved or dispatched requests remain searchable but are not counted as completed journeys.

## Access and responsibilities

- **Requester:** submits and tracks their own requests, then downloads the official ticket when it is sent.
- **Head of Transport Services:** completes the transport review and assigns the vehicle and driver.
- **Director for Administration and President:** review and approve the request at their assigned stages.
- **Administrator:** maintains staff accounts, signatory assignments, and the phone directory.

The system checks access on the server and limits requesters to their own records. Official ticket verification depends on the server's private signing key and the saved approved request.
