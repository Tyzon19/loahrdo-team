# Leave applications

The employee entry point is **Request leave** on `/leaves`; applications, recommending approvals and HR reviews are at `/leaves/requests`. Attendance authentication remains separate from other systems.

## Activate

1. Run `node scripts/migrate-attendance.mjs` with a migration account (`DB_MIGRATION_USER` / `DB_MIGRATION_PASSWORD`) allowed to create tables. The migration is additive and repeatable.
2. HR signs into Leave & Attendance, opens **Leave Applications → HR settings: employee reporting lines**, and assigns every employee’s actual immediate supervisor. The coverage table shows who is still missing a supervisor. Mark department heads there if their existing account role is not already a head role. Existing department heads, transport heads and directors always route to the existing President account. Ordinary employees use their HR-assigned supervisor; the unique department-head fallback is only for accounts that have not been assigned yet. HRDO is recognized as the abbreviation of Human Resources Development Office. Missing or ambiguous reviewers block submission rather than routing to an arbitrary person.
3. In the existing Google Apps Script attendance project, either replace the attendance-sync source with `scripts/Code.gs` (add `NON_TEACHING_SPREADSHEET_ID`, `COLLEGE_SPREADSHEET_ID` or `BASIC_ED_SPREADSHEET_ID`; existing `HRDO_WEBHOOK_URL` and `HRDO_WEBHOOK_SECRET` are also read), or keep the current attendance file and add `scripts/LeaveApprovalSync.gs` as a new file. The compatibility file reuses the existing `hrdoAttendanceConfig_()` and does not replace the attendance push. Run **installLeaveApprovalTrigger** once and authorize Google if prompted. This adds a one-minute `syncApprovedLeaves` timer; existing edit/change triggers remain intact. Run **syncApprovedLeaves** manually for an immediate check.
4. The existing `SITE_URL` and Gmail OAuth configuration are reused. Email outbox entries retry during leave submissions, decisions and the one-minute script polling. HR can also click **Retry email delivery**. A notification links to the authenticated signed form; medical attachments and signatures are not emailed to unrelated recipients.

## Behavior

- Employee → assigned supervisor → HR. Department head → President/CEO → HR. The employee cannot self-approve; HR cannot skip recommending approval. HR may approve or reject, with their name and signature recorded. Rejection requires remarks.
- The employee checks only the actual dates being requested; no dates are preselected. Requests are limited to January–December of the STAFF calendar year in A2; A1 only selects the displayed month. Dates marked non-working by the synced STAFF row 3 formulas are disabled and rechecked at submission and HR approval. Overlapping pending/approved applications and existing attendance entries are blocked. Rejected requests release their reserved dates. A medical certificate is required for two or more sick-leave dates; a certificate can also be attached for one day.
- The current STAFF snapshot is authoritative for its January–December calendar year. New and existing leave/attendance entries are synced from it; entries removed from the sheet are deactivated without deleting history.
- Submitted applications retain their assigned reviewer when HR edits reporting lines. Names, signatures, decisions, remarks and dates are retained in the application. No existing account role is changed by reporting-line settings.
- The form follows LOA-HRDO-LF-034 revision 01, including the notes, recommending signature sections and HR fields. Print / save PDF uses browser printing. The application date, requested dates and immutable reference appear on the form.
- Credited HR-approved leave queues a tracker update. The script validates the A2 calendar year, row-3 working-day flag, configured spreadsheet, employee row, every date cell and existing notes before writing to that date's January–December block. It preserves formulas and conflicting attendance. Each date is marked with the application ID so interrupted jobs can retry. Updated formulas are pushed back through the existing authenticated snapshot sync before the application is marked synced. Utilized SL/VL comes from the tracker's breakup columns, including half days; remaining balances come from the accepted snapshot. Those balances are then frozen on the signed receipt.
- **Not credited:** HR approval and signatures are saved, but the application is never written to the tracker and no SL/VL balance is changed. This is the deliberate policy because the supplied tracker has no established unpaid-leave code: the system does not invent one, substitute a paid-leave code, or use `A`/`ABSENT` (which would mislabel approved leave as an unexcused absence). HR records the dates using the institution’s established non-crediting process. The receipt shows **Not credited — no SL/VL deduction**; balances are not invented. Not-credited jobs are excluded from the Apps Script queue, so this policy does not depend on the Apps Script installation.
- The template’s filing deadlines are shown as policy notes; they do not automatically reject late or emergency applications. Reviewers decide those cases.

## Verification and limitations

`node scripts/test-attendance-db.mjs` exercises actual SQL and workflow transitions in a disposable database. `vitest run src/test/leaves.test.ts src/test/leave-tracker-script.test.ts` covers input validation, routing, not-credited exclusion, cell offsets, conflicts and retries. Emails are mocked in integration tests; they do not send test leave notices to staff.

Gmail delivery is at-least-once: a crash after Google accepts a message but before the database records success may produce a duplicate notification on retry. Approval records and date reservations are transactional. Script locks serialize this script’s jobs, but cannot stop someone manually editing a sheet while a job runs; conflicting entries should be reconciled by HR. Lists show the latest 200 visible applications. No destructive rollback migration is needed; retain the tables to preserve records.
